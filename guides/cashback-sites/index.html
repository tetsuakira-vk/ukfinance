Best Cashback Sites UK Compared

Cashback sites pay you money every time you buy something through their links. The retailer pays the site a commission for referring you, and the site shares that commission with you. It's completely free to join and use — and the amounts add up significantly over a year.

How much can you earn? Regular users typically earn £200–£500/year. Heavy users (who route all major purchases through cashback) can earn £800+/year.

The Top UK Cashback Sites

TopCashback

Consistently offers the highest cashback rates. Free to join. Rates typically 10–30% higher than competitors on the same retailer. Over 5,000 retailers. Insurance switching can pay £50–£100 cashback per policy; broadband and energy switching regularly pays £50–£100.

Quidco

The second-largest UK cashback site. Good competition with TopCashback — rates vary by retailer so worth checking both. Has a premium membership (£5/year) that unlocks higher rates, but the free tier is fine for most people.

TopCashback vs Quidco — Which is Better?

There's no single winner — rates vary by retailer and change constantly. The smart move is to sign up to both (it's free) and check which is paying more before each purchase.

Cashback Credit Cards

A cashback credit card earns you money on everything you spend — even when you're not buying through a cashback site. The key rule: always clear the balance in full every month or the interest will wipe out the cashback.

  • American Express Platinum Cashback Everyday — 5% for first 3 months, 0.5–1% after. No annual fee.
  • Chase UK — 1% cashback via debit card for 12 months (no credit check needed)

Other Cashback Apps Worth Using

  • Shopmium — cashback on specific branded grocery products in supermarkets
  • Airtime Rewards — links to your card and earns cashback automatically. Cash goes off your phone bill
  • TopCashback In-Store — link your card to earn cashback in physical shops
Patience required: Most cashback takes 1–3 months to track and confirm before you can withdraw. The site waits until the purchase can no longer be returned or disputed — this is normal.
