Best Bank Switching Bonuses UK

Banks regularly pay cash bonuses of £100 to £200 to attract new customers. With the Current Account Switch Service (CASS), switching takes just 7 working days and is completely guaranteed — all your payments, direct debits, and standing orders move automatically.

Free money alert: You can switch multiple times and collect multiple bonuses. Some people earn £400–£600/year just from bank switching. The switch guarantee protects you if anything goes wrong.

How Bank Switching Works

  1. Open a new account with the bank offering the bonus
  2. Request a switch using the Current Account Switch Service (CASS)
  3. Your old account closes, all payments move across automatically within 7 working days
  4. Meet the qualifying requirements (usually: pay in a set amount each month, use the debit card X times)
  5. Bonus paid — usually within 30 days of meeting the criteria

Tips for Maximising Switch Bonuses

  • Read the eligibility criteria carefully — most exclude existing customers or people who've recently had an account with that brand
  • Salary accounts work well — use the new account for your salary to hit the pay-in requirement easily
  • The switch guarantee is legally binding — if any payment goes missing, the bank must reimburse you immediately
  • Check your credit file — opening multiple accounts can cause a small, temporary dip. Space switches out if you're applying for a mortgage soon
