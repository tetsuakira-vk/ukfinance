If you're thinking about switching broadband providers, Virgin Media has just made the decision a bit more tempting. The company is offering new customers a free Ultra HD 4K smart TV worth around £250, or a £125 bill credit instead — but you'll need to act fast.

The catch? This offer is only available until the end of this month, so if you're interested, time is of the essence.

Who can get this deal?

The offer is aimed at people who currently use Sky, BT, or TalkTalk. If you're with one of these providers and have been thinking about making a switch, Virgin Media is actively trying to win you over with this sweetener. The free TV is a proper Ultra HD 4K model, which would normally cost you a fair bit if you bought it separately.

Should you switch?

Before you jump at this offer, it's worth doing some homework. Compare Virgin Media's actual broadband packages, speeds, and monthly costs against what you're paying now. A free TV is nice, but it won't matter much if you end up paying significantly more each month for slower speeds.

Ask yourself:

- What speeds do you actually need?
- How much will the monthly bill be?
- Are there any early exit fees if you leave Sky, BT, or TalkTalk early?
- How reliable is Virgin Media in your area? Check independent reviews and Ofcom complaints data.

If switching saves you money overall or gives you better speeds for a similar price, and the free TV is genuinely a bonus on top, then it could be a smart move for your household budget.

Act now if interested

The deadline is the end of this month, so if you want to take advantage, don't leave it too long. Head to Virgin Media's website, enter your postcode to check availability, and compare the deal against your current provider.

Remember: a shiny new TV is only good value if the underlying service and price work for you too.
