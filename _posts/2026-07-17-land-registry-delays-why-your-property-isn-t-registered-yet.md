If you've just completed on a house purchase, you might be shocked to hear that it could take up to six months or longer for the Land Registry to officially register your ownership. But here's the reassuring bit: you don't need to panic.

The Land Registry is currently working through a significant backlog left over from the Covid pandemic. Six to twelve-month delays are not unusual at the moment. However, your legal rights are actually protected much earlier than you might think — the moment your solicitor submits the application to register, not when it's finally processed.

Before you completed your purchase, your solicitor should have filed what's called an "Official Search" or "priority search". This is the key protection. It means that once priority is granted, no one else can jump the queue and register a claim against the property while yours is being processed. Your ownership claim is effectively secured from that point onwards, even if the paperwork takes months to come through.

The Land Registry has made progress. In 2023, the worst-case wait time peaked at 20 months. Now, 95 per cent of applications are processed within nine months — still a wait, but improving. Last year alone, the Registry completed over 213,000 more applications than the previous year, showing real momentum in tackling the backlog.

What should you do right now? Keep on top of progress by chasing your solicitor periodically, especially if you're planning any future moves — like remortgaging, selling the property on, or letting it out. If delays would genuinely cause you a problem, you can request an expedited (fast-track) service from the Land Registry, which is available free of charge.

For straightforward home purchases where you're simply moving in and settling down, delays shouldn't cause real concern. Your ownership is protected, and you can continue with your mortgage and life as normal.

If you're worried about the status of your registration, contact your solicitor directly or speak to the Land Registry's customer service team for an update on your specific case.
