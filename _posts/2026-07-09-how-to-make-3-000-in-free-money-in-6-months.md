Making extra cash without spending your own money might sound too good to be true — but it's entirely possible if you know where to look. One personal finance writer managed to make over £3,000 in just six months by using legitimate money-making tactics that are available to most UK adults.

The key? Spending just 15 minutes a day on activities that put cash back in your pocket. This isn't about get-rich-quick schemes or anything dodgy — it's about maximising the rewards and freebies that already exist.

Here are some realistic ways you can do the same:

**Cashback and rewards schemes.** Many credit card providers, supermarkets, and shopping websites offer cashback on everyday purchases. If you're already spending money on groceries and essentials, you might as well earn a percentage back. Just make sure you're not overspending to chase rewards.

**Signup bonuses.** Banks, investment apps, and financial services frequently offer welcome bonuses for new customers. These can range from £20 to several hundred pounds. Always read the small print — some have conditions like maintaining a minimum balance for a certain period.

**Online surveys and market research.** Companies pay for your opinions. While individual payments are small (usually £1–5 per survey), they add up over time and require minimal effort.

**Cashback websites.** Before shopping online, use a cashback portal. You'll earn a percentage back on purchases from major retailers — completely free to join.

**Energy switching.** If you haven't switched suppliers recently, you could make significant savings. Comparison sites often offer cashback on top of lower tariffs.

**Selling unused items.** Apps like Vinted, eBay, or Facebook Marketplace let you turn clutter into cash with minimal effort.

The golden rule: only participate in schemes you genuinely trust, and never spend extra money chasing rewards. The real win is capturing free money that's already available — then putting it towards your savings goals or paying down debt.

Start small with one or two schemes that fit your lifestyle. Even 15 minutes a day of strategic money-making can genuinely add up over six months.
