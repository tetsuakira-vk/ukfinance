If you bank with Santander, you might be about to receive up to £150 in free cash — but only if you meet certain criteria and take action soon.

The banking giant is offering cashback to customers who arrange a new life insurance policy. Here's what you need to know to check if you're eligible and how to claim.

**Who qualifies for the cash?**

All Santander customers arranging a new life insurance policy will receive £100 cashback. However, if you also hold one of the following, you'll get the full £150 instead:

- A Santander mortgage
- A Santander Select account
- A current account from the Santander Edge range

The life insurance policies are provided by Aviva, the UK's largest insurer. Santander has noted that around 98.8% of Aviva life insurance claims were paid out in 2024, which gives some reassurance about the quality of cover.

**How does it work?**

The cashback isn't something you have to claim — it lands directly in your account. The money arrives 280 days (roughly nine months) after you arrange the policy, provided you haven't cancelled it in that time. This is an important detail: if you cancel early, you won't receive the payment.

**Why might you need life insurance?**

Life insurance can be valuable if you're going through major life changes — buying a house, getting married, having children, or renovating your home. It helps protect your loved ones financially if something happens to you. Santander's messaging suggests thinking about your cover as your circumstances change.

**Important things to remember**

Terms and conditions apply to this offer, and Santander can withdraw it at any time. The promotional emails have already been sent out this week, so check your inbox. Also note that monthly premiums won't rise after you take out a policy, which makes budgeting easier.

**What to do next**

If you're interested, review the email from Santander to confirm your eligibility based on your account type. Consider whether life insurance is actually right for your situation — don't be swayed purely by the cashback. Compare cover options carefully and read the full terms before committing.
