If you're feeling the squeeze at the petrol pump, Tesco has a limited-time offer that could put some money back in your pocket — but you need to act fast.

**What's on offer?**

Tesco is giving customers a 5% fuel discount when they spend £60 or more in-store. You'll receive a coupon that cuts 5p off every litre of petrol or diesel at one of Tesco's 600 UK forecourts. The maximum saving is £10 per transaction, which could make a real difference if you fill up a large tank.

The catch? You only have until Sunday, 23 August to use your coupon, so don't let it gather dust in a drawer.

**Why this matters right now**

Fuel prices have climbed to their highest level since November 2022, with petrol averaging 159.89p per litre. Diesel is even pricier at around 179p. These rises follow ongoing geopolitical tensions that have pushed pump prices up by around 27p per litre since earlier this year. For many households already stretched by the cost of living crisis, every penny saved on fuel counts.

**How to get the deal**

The coupon is only available in paper form at Tesco checkouts — so if you're a regular shopper there, keep an eye out when you next pay. You'll need to present it when you pay for fuel. The offer runs until 16 August for getting the coupon, but you can redeem it until 23 August.

If you don't shop at Tesco regularly, it's worth considering a quick £60 shop if it helps you access this saving, especially if you're running low on fuel anyway.

**What you should do**

Check your next Tesco receipt carefully — the coupon might already be there. If you shop at Tesco and drive regularly, this is a straightforward way to cut costs. With fuel prices unlikely to fall dramatically in the near term, taking advantage of any discount available is smart budgeting.

Head to your nearest Tesco and ask staff if you're unsure how to claim the offer.
