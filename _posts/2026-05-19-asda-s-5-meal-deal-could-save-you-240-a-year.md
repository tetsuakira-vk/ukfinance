If you buy lunch at the supermarket regularly, Asda's new "Exceptional" meal deal could put real money back in your pocket — potentially £240 a year or more.

From 18 May, Asda is offering a premium meal deal nationwide (including Express stores) for just £5. You get to pick a main, a snack, and a drink. The range includes 17 quality options like Prosciutto Cotto focaccia, Steak & Onion sandwiches, Beef Birria wraps, and hand-finished sushi rolls.

**How much are you actually saving?**

Asda undercuts its main rivals on premium meal deals. Tesco charges £5.50 with a Clubcard (£6 without), while Sainsbury's and Co-op both charge £5.50 (£6 for non-members at Co-op). That's 50p to £1 less per meal at Asda.

Money-saving blogger Alasdair Baker from The Penny Pincher blog has worked out the real-world impact. If you buy lunch just a few times a week, that 50p–£1 saving adds up fast. Over a working month, you could save £10 to £20. Scale that up across a full year, and you're looking at £120 to £240back in your pocket — depending on how often you buy lunch.

**Which deals offer the best value?**

Baker highlights some standout options: the Chicken Caesar Shake-A-Salad (which costs less in the deal than comparable salads sold separately), the Beef Birria Wrap with dipping sauce, and the deli-style focaccia sandwiches. The sushi options are also worth considering if you like premium lunch choices.

**Why now?**

Asda says this move is part of its wider commitment to improve value for shoppers as food costs continue to rise. With cost-of-living pressures still squeezing household budgets, every saving counts — and lunch is an easy place to cut costs without sacrificing quality.

If you're in the habit of buying a supermarket lunch several times a week, it's worth switching to your nearest Asda to take advantage of this deal. Small savings on everyday purchases genuinely do add up over time.
