If you've got children heading back to school this September, you'll know that uniform costs can add up quickly. The good news is that Aldi has brought back its popular £5 school uniform bundle – a real lifeline for parents feeling the squeeze on household budgets.

The discount supermarket is offering a five-piece uniform set for just £5 per child, making it one of the cheapest options on the high street. This bundle typically includes essentials like polo shirts, trousers or skirts, and a school jumper – the core items most schools require. With the average family spending £150–£200 per child on uniforms alone, Aldi's deal can make a genuine difference to your back-to-school bill.

For many UK families, back-to-school shopping ranks alongside Christmas as one of the year's biggest spending events. When you factor in uniforms, PE kits, shoes, bags, and stationery, costs can easily spiral beyond £300 per child. That's why spotting these kinds of deals matters, especially if you're juggling multiple children or already stretched by rising living costs.

To make the most of this offer, act quickly. Aldi's special buys (including seasonal offers like school uniforms) sell out fast, and availability varies by store. Pop into your local branch or check the Aldi website to see if stock is available near you. It's worth visiting early in the day if you can, as popular items tend to shift rapidly.

Beyond Aldi, don't overlook other budget-friendly options. Many supermarkets – including Tesco, Sainsbury's and Asda – also offer competitive uniform ranges. School uniform swap shops and Facebook community groups are increasingly popular too, letting parents buy and sell pre-loved uniforms at a fraction of the retail price.

If you're really struggling with back-to-school costs, remember that some local councils and charities offer grants or vouchers to help low-income families. It's worth contacting your local authority or checking whether you qualify for help through programmes in your area.

The key to managing back-to-school spending is planning ahead and comparing prices across retailers. Aldi's £5 bundle is a smart starting point – just remember to get in quickly before stocks run out.
