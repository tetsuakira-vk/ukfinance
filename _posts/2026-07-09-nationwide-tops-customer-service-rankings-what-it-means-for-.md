If you're thinking about switching banks or building society, here's some good news: Nationwide Building Society has just topped the UK's official customer satisfaction rankings for the first time ever, scoring 87.3 out of 100.

This matters because choosing where to keep your money is one of the most important financial decisions you'll make. A building society or bank with strong customer service means better support when things go wrong, easier access to your branches, and generally a smoother experience managing your money.

**Why Nationwide won**

Nationwide's rise to the top reflects several decisions that appeal to everyday savers and borrowers. The building society has committed to keeping all its branches open until at least 2030 – increasingly rare as many high street banks shut theirs. This is particularly valued in smaller towns where Nationwide is often the last branch left.

Nationwide is also known for its Fairer Share scheme, which distributes profits back to members. This year, around 4.4 million eligible members received £100 each – totalling £440 million. This genuinely puts money back in your pocket simply for being a member.

**The bigger picture**

For the first time in the UK Customer Satisfaction Index's 18-year history, banks and building societies as a sector have overtaken retail for customer service quality. This is encouraging news – it suggests the financial services industry is finally listening to complaints and improving how they treat customers.

Other top performers included John Lewis (second place with 87.1) and First Direct (third with 86.0), so you have genuinely good options if you're looking to switch.

**What you should do**

If you're unhappy with your current bank or building society, this ranking is a useful starting point. Visit the Institute of Customer Service website to see the full top 10 list and read detailed feedback from real customers. Consider whether Nationwide's branch commitment and member benefits align with how you want to bank.

Don't just switch for rankings though – check whether their savings rates, loan deals, and accounts genuinely suit your needs first.
