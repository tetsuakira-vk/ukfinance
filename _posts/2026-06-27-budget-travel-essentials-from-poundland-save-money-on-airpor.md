If you're planning a holiday this year, here's a money-saving tip that could cut your airport shopping bill: Poundland has become an unlikely hero for budget-conscious travellers.

A savvy shopper recently highlighted that Poundland stocks travel-size toiletries — including 100ml bottles of suncream and shampoo — that meet strict airport security rules. This matters because airport shops are notorious for charging premium prices for essentials you've forgotten or run out of.

Why does this matter to your holiday budget? Airport retailers typically mark up travel-size items by 200–300% compared to high street prices. A 100ml bottle of sunscreen that costs £1 in Poundland could easily cost £3–4 at the airport. Over a week's holiday with a family, that adds up quickly.

The key advantage here is the 100ml size. UK airport security rules limit liquids in hand luggage to containers no larger than 100ml, and these must fit in a single clear plastic bag. This means you can't bring a full-size bottle through security — so travellers often either go without or pay inflated airport prices. Shopping at Poundland before you travel solves this problem cheaply.

This is genuinely useful consumer advice if you're shopping on a tight budget. It's worth checking what Poundland stocks near you before your trip — items vary by location. You might find other travel essentials too, from chargers to travel pillows.

The broader lesson here is simple: airports are expensive. Whether it's food, drinks, or toiletries, you'll always pay more once you're past security. Doing a bit of prep shopping at discount chains like Poundland, Home Bargains, or The Range can genuinely save you £10–20 per person on a typical week-long trip.

Before you travel, make a quick checklist of what you need in your carry-on, then pop to your local discount retailer. Your wallet will thank you when you're relaxing on the beach instead of fretting about holiday costs.
