If you hold a Blue Badge, pay attention. Over 450 permits have just been cancelled in a fraud crackdown, and the rules around how you use yours are stricter than many people realise.

The joint investigation between the Public Sector Fraud Authority and Wirral Council uncovered a worrying trend: people continuing to use Blue Badges after the original holder had died. It's an easy mistake to make, but it's also a criminal offence—and one that's cost taxpayers over £363,000 between 2024 and 2025. Traffic wardens in the Wirral are now carrying out targeted patrols with the power to confiscate badges on the spot.

**What you absolutely cannot do**

The biggest misconception is that you can lend your Blue Badge to friends or family. You cannot. The moment you hand your badge to someone else—even a close relative—you're breaking the law. If your partner or grown-up child needs to use it, they must be present in the vehicle as a passenger. The badge is not transferable, and misusing it (whether you're the badge holder or someone else using it without permission) can result in formal warnings, confiscation, and fines up to £1,000.

You also cannot copy your badge, change the details on it, or continue using it after your circumstances have changed.

**Getting the basics right**

Display your badge correctly: it must sit on your dashboard where it's clearly visible through the front windscreen, with the hologram facing upwards. If it's worn, faded, or illegible, you need to apply for a replacement—don't just keep using it.

**When to return your badge**

Hand your badge back to your local council if it expires, your condition improves, you're issued a replacement, it becomes damaged or illegible, or if the holder passes away or becomes permanently housebound.

The scheme exists to help people with disabilities and limited mobility access fairer parking. Using it correctly protects both your rights and the integrity of the system. If you're unsure about anything, visit Gov.uk for full guidance or contact your local council directly.
