With temperatures soaring and energy bills already a serious concern for many UK households, Lidl's latest range of affordable cooling gadgets could help you stay comfortable without pushing your budget to breaking point.

The supermarket has launched a collection of cooling devices ahead of the Met Office's red heat warnings this week, with temperatures expected to reach 38°C in some parts of the UK. If you're worried about the cost of running air conditioning or fans for hours on end, Lidl's new range offers some genuinely budget-friendly options.

**The star of the show is the TRONIC Portable Air Cooler at £14.99.** This handheld device cools and humidifies using pure water from its 700ml tank and promises to keep running for up to seven hours on a single fill. It has three air speed settings, a practical carry handle, and even includes a colour-changing LED mood light as a bonus. For this price point, it's worth checking out if you need portable cooling for a bedroom or living room.

If you need something more powerful, Lidl is also stocking the TRONIC 3-in-1 Air Conditioner for £149. This combines air conditioning, fan cooling and dehumidifying in one unit with transport wheels, making it a serious investment but potentially worthwhile if you're facing a long, hot summer.

The full Lidl cooling range also includes tower fans from £17.99, desk fans from £14.99, and even cooling pillows and mats for as little as £3.99. These budget alternatives might be worth considering if you want to keep bills down while staying comfortable.

**A word of caution:** portable coolers and fans can help circulate air, but they're not a replacement for proper air conditioning if you have health vulnerabilities or live in an extremely hot environment. Check what's suitable for your needs.

**What you need to do:** Head to your nearest Lidl store to check stock availability – home delivery isn't available for these items. Visit Lidl's website for full product details and your local store's opening hours. If you're struggling with energy costs more broadly, Citizens Advice offers free guidance on managing bills and staying cool safely during heatwaves.
