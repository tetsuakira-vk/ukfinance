The July 2026 Premium Bonds prize draw has been announced, with one lucky winner taking home £1 million. If you hold Premium Bonds, it's worth checking whether you've had a windfall.

Premium Bonds are a savings product run by NS&I (National Savings & Investments), which is backed by the UK Treasury. Unlike traditional savings accounts that pay interest, Premium Bonds work differently: instead of earning money on your balance, you're automatically entered into a monthly prize draw for a chance to win cash prizes ranging from £25 up to £1 million.

**How Premium Bonds work**

When you invest in Premium Bonds, you buy them in £1 denominations. Each £1 bond gets a unique number, and every month NS&I holds a draw where winning numbers are selected at random. If your number comes up, you win a prize — but your original £1 stays with NS&I unless you decide to cash in your bonds.

The appeal is clear: you could win a substantial sum while your money remains safe and guaranteed by the government. However, there's an important catch. Most people won't win anything in any given month. It's a gamble, and the odds of winning the top prize are extremely slim — around 1 in 66.9 billion per bond per draw.

**Is Premium Bonds right for you?**

Premium Bonds can be a fun way to save small amounts, especially if you enjoy the idea of a potential big win. However, if you need your savings to grow reliably, a fixed-rate savings account or a Cash ISA will give you guaranteed returns. With interest rates still relatively decent, you might earn more through traditional savings than you would through Premium Bonds over time.

**Check if you've won**

You can check your Premium Bonds online through the NS&I website using your holder's number. NS&I will also write to you if you've won a prize of £500 or more, so keep an eye on your post.

To find out more about Premium Bonds or to invest, visit the NS&I website at nsandi.com.
