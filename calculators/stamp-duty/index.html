2025 Rates at a Glance

England & NI (SDLT) — Standard buyer:
0% up to £125,000 • 2% £125k–£250k • 5% £250k–£925k • 10% £925k–£1.5m • 12% above £1.5m
First-time buyers (England & NI):
0% up to £300,000 • 5% £300k–£500k • Standard rates above £500,000
Additional properties:
Standard rates + 3% surcharge on each band
